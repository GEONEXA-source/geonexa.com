GeoNEXA AI

Connecting the World, Spatial Sense — AI-powered land, property, and spatial risk intelligence for Rwanda.

What GeoNEXA does

GeoNEXA AI is a land and property intelligence platform built for Rwanda. It combines interactive mapping, real parcel data, and AI-assisted analysis so individuals, engineers, surveyors, and institutions can make more informed land and property decisions.

Core features

🗺️
Map Explorer
Search and browse Rwanda's land parcels on an interactive map.
🤖
AI Assistant
Ask questions about a parcel, zoning, or spatial risk and get an AI-grounded answer.
📄
Reports
Generate downloadable reports on parcels and locations.
❤
Saved Properties
Bookmark parcels and reports to revisit anytime.
🧭
Engineer Panel
A dedicated workspace for engineers and surveyors.
💬
Team Chat
Engineers and surveyors can chat and share updates.

Who built this

GeoNEXA AI was founded by a team of students at the University of Rwanda, bringing together expertise in Surveying and Geomatics Engineering with modern software development. The team's academic grounding in land surveying and spatial analysis directly shapes how GeoNEXA approaches parcel data, zoning, and risk assessment — built by people who understand the domain, not just the code.
